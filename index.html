NARRATIVE CINEMA & ARTISTIC ROOTS

A Lifelong Passion for Cinematic Storytelling

Mehedi Hasan Bappy's journey is anchored in independent auteur cinema, driven by a deep fascination with psychological fragility, subtle human subtext, and the meticulous geometry of 35mm composition.

His breakthrough short film Kuhok gained international critical acclaim, winning at the Clapperboard Golden Fest Brazil. He followed with Chakri, honored as Best Short Film at CYFE and selected for the prestigious Duemila30 Film Festival in Milan, Italy. These productions established his distinctive directorial signature: emotionally charged, visually arresting, and uncompromisingly authentic.

VENTURE LEADERSHIP & APPLIED TECHNOLOGY

Scaling Startups & Engineering Real-World Impact

Driven by the conviction that modern storytelling lives across interactive products and real-world logistics, Mehedi expanded into technology entrepreneurship. As co-founder of Groccii Limited, he led the venture in pioneering 10-minute quick-commerce delivery across urban Bangladesh—building end-to-end dark-store fulfillment networks, dynamic inventory management systems, and high-conversion mobile applications.

Groccii's rapid scale was documented across premier global financial and venture intelligence platforms including PitchBook, Tracxn, and The Business Standard (TBS News), demonstrating his ability to govern high-growth operations alongside creative direction.

AI CREATIVE STRATEGY & MOTIONHIKE

Pioneering AI Creative Workflows & High-CTR Media

As the founder of MotionHike, Mehedi designs next-generation AI video pipelines and performance-marketing frameworks. By integrating physical cinema captures with neural diffusion algorithms, synthetic actors, and ComfyUI generation nodes, he produces viral UGC and commercial ads that achieve up to 4x CTR multipliers for global e-commerce and enterprise brands.
